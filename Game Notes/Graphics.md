# Overall look and feel

I want the game to look "geometrical", with most objects being comprised of large smooth solid shapes, to give a simple yet interesting atheistic.

It will fell a bit like low Polly, except mostly not made of triangles, more rounded cubes, prisms, polyhedra etc.

# Space

## Planets

Basically balls made of some sort of hexagon / voronoi pattern as their surface.
This can be quickly and procedurally generated in the frag shader for minimal memory usage, perfect for having many planets with unique characteristics as each one basically just requires an int to seed hash functions.


## Sun
With the sun I want to give the feeling that it's spewing energy out to the surrounding area.
This means making it glow as well as adding volumetric effects such as light shafts and fog

## Background / Interstellar
A dense galaxy, should include many large structures to pride visual interest as well as light sources for the objects in the scene

## The black hole
I think it would be cool to have a large visible black hole at the center of the galaxy, that would serve as a kind of visual compass and way-point
